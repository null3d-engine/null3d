Third-party code in the null3D Vite plugin

The @null3d/vite-plugin package ships a WebAssembly module, dist/shader-compiler.wasm, which the
build runs to compile the engine's WGSL. The module is built from the null3D crates and the Rust
crates below. Each section names the crates, their licenses, and the notices that a license asks a
copy to carry. The npm packages that the plugin depends on carry their own licenses.

The module also holds the text of every WGSL file in the engine's shader library
(crates/null3d-shaders/wgsl and its lib folder), so the projects whose code those shaders port
need their notices here too. They are named first.

==============================================================================
three.js: the shaders of the engine's shader library that its code ports
==============================================================================

The compiler holds the engine's ports of three.js 0.186.1's code, from
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js: the GTAO and Poisson denoise passes, the wide line shaders,
the atmosphere and sky, the trace of the built-in room, and the coat, sheen, iridescence,
refraction and cutout code.

Copyright © 2010-2026 three.js authors

The MIT License, whose text is below.

==============================================================================
Filament: the specular anti-aliasing kernel, the anisotropic highlight and AgX's punchy look
==============================================================================

The engine's shader library follows Filament's specular anti-aliasing kernel, its anisotropic GGX
highlight and the two constants of AgX's punchy look, from https://github.com/google/filament.

Copyright 2023 The Android Open Source Project

Apache License, Version 2.0, whose text is below.

==============================================================================
KinoBokeh and Unity's post-processing stack v1: the depth of field
==============================================================================

The engine's depth-of-field shader ports KinoBokeh, by Keijiro Takahashi, from
https://github.com/keijiro/KinoBokeh, and follows the composite of Unity's post-processing stack
v1's depth of field, from https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/PostProcessing.

KinoBokeh: Copyright (C) 2016 Unity Technologies and Copyright (C) 2015 Keijiro Takahashi
Unity's post-processing stack v1: Copyright (c) 2014-2017, Unity Technologies

The MIT License, whose text is below.

==============================================================================
AMD FidelityFX: FSR 1's RCAS sharpening and SSSR's ray march
==============================================================================

The engine's final shader translates FidelityFX Super Resolution 1's RCAS filter, and its
screen-space reflection shader follows the structure of FidelityFX Stochastic Screen Space
Reflections, both from https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects.

Copyright (c) 2021 Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. All rights reserved.

The MIT License, whose text is below.

==============================================================================
Qualcomm Snapdragon Game Super Resolution 1: the render-scale upscale
==============================================================================

The engine's final shader ports Snapdragon Game Super Resolution 1's fragment shader, from
https://github.com/SnapdragonStudios/snapdragon-gsr, keeping Qualcomm's lanczos window, weighted
taps, edge vote and delta clamp.

Copyright (c) 2023, Qualcomm Innovation Center, Inc. All rights reserved.

BSD 3-Clause License, whose text is below.

==============================================================================
naga, naga-types and naga_oil: WGSL parsing, validation and translation
==============================================================================

naga 30.0.1, naga-types 30.0.1 and naga_oil 0.23.0, from https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu and
https://github.com/bevyengine/naga_oil. Each is offered under the MIT License or the Apache
License 2.0, and null3D takes them under the MIT License.

Copyright the gfx-rs developers and the naga_oil contributors.
