Third-party code in @null3d/three-compat

The @null3d/three-compat package ships code from the projects below. Each section names the files
that hold the code, its licence, and the notices that the licence asks a copy to carry.

A build with the null3D Vite plugin writes this text into null3d-third-party-notices.txt beside
the page, after the engine's own notices. Keep that file with the build when you publish it, or
show its text in your game's credits.

==============================================================================
three.js: the Reinhard and Cineon tone curves, and the OutlinePass effect
==============================================================================

Ports of three.js 0.186.1, https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js: ReinhardToneMapping and
CineonToneMapping from src/renderers/shaders/ShaderChunk/tonemapping_pars_fragment.glsl.js, and
the edge detection, gaussian blur, pulse and overlay of examples/jsm/postprocessing/OutlinePass.js.
