Third-party code in the null3D command-line tool

The @null3d/cli package ships code from the projects below, in its vendor folder and in its asset
module, dist/assets.wasm. Each section names the files that hold the code, its licence, and the
notices that the licence asks a copy to carry. The npm packages that the tool depends on carry
their own licences.
