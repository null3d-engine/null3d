The `governor` plan runs the quality governor's stress test on each GPU path in two stages, in the default thread mode. The walk forces every live step down and back up while the sketch spins its thread for two frame budgets a frame, captures a frame after each step, and fails on a frame that sticks or a pipeline that builds. The hold grows a plane's loop until the GPU draws under 75% of the target, then lets the governor bring the rate back: it fails when the render scale never drops, when the rate does not come back, or when the scale changes direction more than twice in its last ten seconds, which is what flapping looks like.
