## What the figures say

Both forms held the page's update rate at every count. On WebGL2 the pages updated 63 to 71 times a
second, with a CPU median of 1.82 to 1.88 ms. The state pass costs 0.36 ms on core WebGPU at 500 and 2,000
leaves, and 0.08 ms in compatibility mode. The scene's opaque pass, which draws the leaves, moves
0.016 ms from 500 to 10,000 offset leaves there, so the per-leaf field read is cheap.

The GPU timer's whole-frame median on Safari includes idle gaps of 0.7 to 23.6 s between passes on
several pages. Those medians therefore do not compare the forms, and the tables give the pass
medians instead. The state form is the default because it carries a gust along a leaf's path. The
offset form is the cheaper one for leaves that the air only pushes where they stand.
