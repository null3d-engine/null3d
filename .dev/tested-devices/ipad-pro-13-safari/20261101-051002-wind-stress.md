device's WebGPU path, at the device's own window size. The display ran at 59 Hz, so its interval is

Result: 4 passed, 0 skipped, 0 failed. One page per preset, each drawing 500,000 cards as its load.

measures three phases: no cards, all the cards, and no cards again after 20 s. It reads the wind's
resolved settings and its share of the budget 1.5 s into the light phase and 4 s into the heavy
one. It then captures two frames a second apart at 10,000 cards, with the governor still stepped,
so the pixels show the air still moves the cards.

## What the figures say

Every preset with a field drove the governor down. At Medium and High the share fell from 1 to 0.75
and then 0.5, the interval doubled from 33 to 66 and 132 ms, the pressure count fell from 16 to 12
and 8 at Medium and from 24 to 18 and 12 at High, and the spring trees from 8 to 6 and 4 at Medium
and from 32 to 24 and 16 at High. Ultra fell to 0.5 and then 0.25, with the interval at 64 and 128
ms, the pressure count at 20 and 10, and the spring trees at 32 and 16.

The heavy phase's interval is 19.8 to 33.1 ms against the display's 15.7, so the load took every
preset past the frame.

The settle phase returned the interval to 15.6 or 15.7 ms at every preset, inside the display's
interval. The budget share stayed at 0.75, because the governor raises a step slowly and was still
raising one. The frame was inside the budget while the field stayed lightened.

The two captured frames differ in 0.010 to 0.012 per cent of their channels at the presets with a
field. The camera does not move, so only the air can turn the cards. The field therefore keeps
moving while the governor is stepped.

D-283 carries the rows and the budget, and its M3-V16 addendum the governor's floor.
