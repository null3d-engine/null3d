This phone stands in for the S24 class. The Galaxy S24 has no WebGPU adapter and reports no WebGL2
GPU timer, so no run of that device gives a phone GPU time. The Galaxy S25 has both paths, on
Qualcomm's Adreno 830, a faster GPU of another make than the S24's Samsung Xclipse 940. Its row is
the stand-in, not the S24's own figure.

The `lod-cost` page draws 40,000 instanced pines, four levels each, over hills under the sun's
shadows. It times two seconds with every tree at its base mesh and two seconds with each tree at
the level that its size on the screen picks, three times each way. The table gives the median GPU
time of a frame where the device has a GPU timer, the median frame interval, the CPU time, and the

The display runs at 30 Hz, so its frame interval caps at 33.3 ms. Every interval sits at that cap
except High on core WebGPU, where the base-meshes side missed the cap at 66.6 ms. That fall to
33.3 ms is the display's cap catching a frame that was slower than the cap, not the levels' doing.
The runner marks these timings unreliable, and a reader must not take that interval row as a
saving. The GPU time is the figure that shows the saving.
