Plans: Battle's ramp and three counts in both modes against three.js, 12 October 2026, from the branch of M2-EX6 at a0bb42016 ([D-121](../../decisions/D-121-comparison-tier.md#battles-figures-on-the-mac))

Result: production builds in a Chrome 155 window that Playwright opened on the Mac's built-in display at 120 Hz. The canvas was 1280 x 720 CSS pixels at pixel ratio 1, with 4x MSAA and every effect on. Each page started at a 1-minute load below 8. The ramps had room for 40,000 soldiers and mechs, started at 200 and grew by a fifth at each step. A step held at 114 frames a second or more. Each count warmed up for 4 seconds and measured 10, with room for that count only.

Scene graph mode. Held, then frames a second and the busiest thread's CPU time per frame at 1,000, 4,000 and 10,000 units, then the page's memory at 4,000:
- null3D on WebGPU, High: held 3,698. 64.6 fps (3.60 ms), 42.4 fps (18.6 ms) and 58.2 fps (12.1 ms). 516 MiB.
- null3D on WebGL2, Medium: held 200. 55.8 fps (3.15 ms), 30.0 fps (13.0 ms) and 26.8 fps (31.2 ms). 341 MiB.
- three.js WebGLRenderer: held none. 8.0 fps (120 ms), 1.9 fps (531 ms) and 1.0 fps (974 ms). 435 MiB.
- three.js WebGPURenderer: held none. 7.5 fps (128 ms), 1.7 fps (587 ms) and 0.7 fps (1,346 ms). 630 MiB.

Instanced mode, the same figures:
- null3D on WebGPU, High: held 7,668. 64.7 fps (3.51 ms), 53.1 fps (15.4 ms) and 35.8 fps (23.5 ms). 560 MiB.
- null3D on WebGL2, Medium: held none at the full rate, and 240 at half the rate. 54.6 fps (3.12 ms), 26.9 fps (14.5 ms) and 25.8 fps (30.4 ms). 341 MiB.
- three.js WebGPURenderer on WebGPU, the crowd example's route: held 240. 87.5 fps (10.6 ms), 29.3 fps (33.9 ms) and 11.1 fps (86.3 ms). 58 MiB. A second run gave 70.0, 25.1 and 10.3 fps.

The null3D WebGPU point at 4,000 units in the scene graph mode ended at a load of 8.1, and the three.js WebGLRenderer point at 10,000 ended at 8.6. The same points with room for the ramp's top, at loads below 8, gave 43.6 fps and 0.8 fps.
