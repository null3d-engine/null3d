The levels cut the GPU time by 32% at Low and 40% at High on core WebGPU. They cut it by 37% and
43% in compatibility mode, and by 34% and 66% on WebGL2. Chrome on the Mac times WebGL2, which the
tablets and the phone report no timer for. The GPU buffer bytes include the copy lists and are
identical with the levels off and on, as are the textures and the wasm memory. The copy lists
therefore follow what draws ([D-137](../../decisions/D-137-lod-groups.md#addendum-the-copy-lists-follow-what-draws-22-october-2026)).
