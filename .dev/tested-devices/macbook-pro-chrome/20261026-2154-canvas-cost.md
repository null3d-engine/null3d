The page follows a 1024 x 1024 canvas and refreshes a 256 x 256 rectangle of it at 30 Hz. It times
twenty refreshes of the rectangle, then four of the whole canvas, and then measures a window of
frames that refresh the rectangle against the same drawing with no refresh. A run is one path:
`NULL3D_CANVAS_COST=1 bun run test:browser -- canvas-texture.spec.ts --workers=1`.
