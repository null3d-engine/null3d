Plans: Battle in null3D on WebGPU before and after task M2-EX41's shared skinned buckets and memory fixes, 13 October 2026. "Before" is main at 16a710a2f, "after" the branch perf/m2-ex41-battle-gpu ([D-121](../../decisions/D-121-comparison-tier.md#battles-gpu-cost-and-memory-12-october-2026))

Result: production builds in a Chrome 155 window that Playwright opened on the Mac's built-in display at 120 Hz. The canvas was 1280 x 720 CSS pixels at pixel ratio 1, with every effect on, at the High preset. Each page started at a 1-minute load below 8 with the GPU idle. Each count warmed up for 4 seconds and measured 10, with room for that count only. Each count ran twice, before and after in turn, and the 4,000-unit pages once more with Chrome's memory figure. The ramps did not run: they wait for task M2-EX7's fix to the ramp's reading of the display rate.

Frames a second, the busiest thread's CPU time and the GPU's frame time, before and then after:
- Instanced, 1,000 units: 68.1 fps (3.03 ms, 16.8 ms GPU), then 79.1 and 89.9 fps (2.54 and 1.77 ms; 16.7 and 13.9 ms GPU). Draws 10,214, then 333.
- Instanced, 4,000 units: 54.5, 61.1 and 60.5 fps (13.9, 9.25 and 9.81 ms; 13.2 to 14.9 ms GPU), then 67.8, 89.3 and 90.5 fps (9.56, 5.68 and 5.74 ms; 9.7 to 13.7 ms GPU). Draws 10,464, then 343.
- Scene graph, 1,000 units: 60.5 and 67.9 fps (4.44 and 2.92 ms; 12.6 and 17.1 ms GPU), then 91.1 and 93.6 fps (1.72 and 2.85 ms; 13.2 and 12.0 ms GPU).
- Scene graph, 4,000 units: 59.7 and 63.1 fps (10.7 and 8.62 ms; 12.7 and 13.9 ms GPU), then 88.7, 88.7 and 90.1 fps (6.16, 6.02 and 5.83 ms; 13.3 to 13.6 ms GPU).

The page's memory at 4,000 units, with the shared memory counted once, then Chrome's whole figure: instanced 525 MiB (669), then 115 MiB (252); scene graph 567 MiB (710), then 115 MiB (252).

Heap snapshots at 1,000 units, instanced: the render worker's heap 36.2 MiB with 34.1 MiB of strings, then 10.5 MiB with 8.8 MiB. The sketch worker's heap 20.5 MiB with 15.7 MiB of strings, then 15.1 MiB with 10.0 MiB.

The 4,000-unit pages held only 1,024 soldiers, and their scene stood still. The engine's table of animated objects held at most 1,024 objects, so each frame stopped at the first unit past it (found on 13 October 2026, task M2-R36). Their figures compare the two builds on that same scene, not the cost of 4,000 units.

Two "before" pages ended at a load just over 8: instanced at 1,000 units (112.0 fps, 1.89 ms, 8.8 ms GPU) and scene graph at 4,000 (60.7 fps, 10.8 ms, 13.0 ms GPU). The figures above leave them out.
