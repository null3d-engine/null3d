## What the figures say

Every preset with a field stepped down and came back. The step interval doubled at each step to the
133 ms cap or to its own value, the pressure count scaled by the budget's share, and the spring
trees scaled with it. The frame interval returned to the display's 16.67 ms at all four presets,
inside the budget. The budget share came back to 1 at Medium, and to 0.75 at the others, where the
governor was still raising a step.

The heavy load is far past the frame: the GPU takes 169 to 296 ms a frame, so the governor's three
steps all run. No device needs that much load; it is what this Mac needed to leave the display's
cap, and the devices ran a smaller one.

D-283 carries the rows and the budget, and its M3-V16 addendum the governor's floor.
