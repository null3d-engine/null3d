## What the figures say

The field's reader is cheaper than the analytic one in every WebGPU pass that draws a plant. One
texture read replaces the steady field's five curl taps and its transcendentals. The saving is
0.018 to 0.136 ms in the cascades and 0.085 ms in the main view's opaque pass. It doubles in the
motion view, which runs the offset twice. Against that, the field's own pass costs 0.089 ms at
Medium. The whole frame is therefore 0.06 ms dearer there and 0.17 ms at Ultra.

The WebGL2 row does not isolate the reader. The add-on steps at a fixed sixty cycles a second, so a
slower frame runs more cycles. That raises the next frame's field work. At this row the display is
at 26 frames a second, so the pass runs about two cycles a frame. The 20.7 ms therefore mixes the
field's pass with the reader. WebGL2's missing pass timer cannot separate them. It is the reason
Medium takes 15 cycles a second on that path.

The grove is the only dressed plant scene the add-on draws on this tree. Creek's and the forest's
passes, and the reflections, wait on M3-V11 and M3-V12.
