## What the figures say

The whole look costs 3.1 to 3.9 ms at every preset on WebGPU and in compatibility mode, and every one of those eight cells keeps its whole range above zero. The cost follows the canvas's pixels rather than the preset, since the page holds the render scale at 1: about a tenth of a millisecond for each megapixel.

The four marks resolve on every path, between 0.19 and 0.70 ms. The color fringes' compatibility cell has a range that touches zero, so that one sits at the edge of the method's reach. The streak on WebGPU and the speed blur in compatibility mode have their whole range clear of zero and are the firmest of the twelve.

The sharpening resolves on WebGPU at 1.32 ms and in compatibility mode at 1.09 ms, which is the first step of the engine's two-step pass that a sharpening value adds at the whole render scale. WebGL2's timer does not separate it from the frame's own movement there.

The grade and the vignette read between -0.38 and 0.14 ms across the paths. They draw inside the final pass, and this method cannot tell them from the frame's own movement: treat them as under about 0.2 ms.

WebGL2's whole-frame timer spans the frame's commands as a whole, and its off side moved between 8.4 and 12.7 ms between cells, so no whole-look figure is stated for that path. Its marks stand, because eight times a mark rises above that movement.

The first run's whole-look cells sit inside the second run's ranges on WebGPU, and its WebGL2 cells disagree from run to run: 1.74 and 3.18 at Low, 1.74 and -0.91 at Medium.
