## The chosen slot and its fold

The engine times seven passes one by one, and the seventh also counts every pass after it. Both
fringes cells read `bloom 4`, the seventh and folded slot. The add-on's effect joins the final pass,
which lies inside the folded part, so the slot's name describes only the first pass in it. The
figure still belongs to the lens: the two sides draw the same folded passes and differ only by the
mark, so the difference is the mark's. That is an ablation, not an attribution.

The sharpening cells read the whole frame, because WebGL2 times no pass, so no fold applies to them.
Their figure is the two-step pass's added work together with the frame's own movement, which is why
the Low cell sits at the floor.
