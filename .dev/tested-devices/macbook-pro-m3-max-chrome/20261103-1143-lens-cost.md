## Why a loaded batch reads a cheaper look

Load adds time to a frame, but not to both sides alike here. In the loaded batches the look-off side
inflated from 2.136 to 3.645 ms while the look-on side stayed near 6 ms, so the difference between
the sides shrank and the look appeared to cost less. The cheapest batch, 2.513 ms, is therefore the
loaded one, not the clean one. A figure taken beside other work can look cheaper than the code, and
only a quiet batch tells the two apart.
