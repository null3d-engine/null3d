## What the figures say

The tablet's field is dearer than the iPad's at every shape, and its coarse timer hides the small
steps. The shipped cycle reads 0.262 ms at flat 32x32 and 0.983 at flat 64x64, against the iPad's
0.184 and 0.191. The layered shape's 24 steps cost 5.308 ms against the iPad's 0.578, and the
volume's 40 steps 8.389 against 1.326.

Ultra is the row at the budget's edge here. The field steps at a fixed 60 steps a second, so a frame
of this 11.14 ms display owes two thirds of a cycle. One cycle is about 9.3 ms with the advection
and projection on top, so a frame carries about 6.2 ms, right at the 6.25 ms budget. The canopy drag
adds 1.114 ms a cycle, which takes the frame to about 6.9 ms, over the budget by a tenth.

High's row is the layered shape at 24 steps and 30 field steps a second, which is a third of a cycle
a frame on this display: about 2.1 ms against 4.9. Medium's cycle at 16 steps is about 1.08 ms, so
about 0.36 ms a frame against 1.2.

On WebGL2 the field's cost does not rise above the frame query's floor at either flat shape, and the
interval holds at 11.14 ms. These rows bound the field's cost there rather than measure it.
