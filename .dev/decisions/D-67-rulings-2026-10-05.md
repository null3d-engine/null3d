# D-67: The owner's rulings of 5 October 2026

Status: decided by the owner on 2026-10-05, at about 09:00 Singapore time. Date: 2026-10-05. Task: M2-N1.

## Question

On the night of 4 to 5 October 2026, the M1 gate's device runs and eight M2 tasks raised questions for the owner. They were about the browsers to support and the gate's iPad results. Others were about canvas reuse after `destroy()`, destroy calls for meshes and prefabs, animation clips and bloom. The last were about WebGL2 texture units and the steps of the room's generator. What did the owner rule, and where is each ruling recorded?

## Rule

The owner decides. Each ruling keeps the rules that hold already. They are [D-52](D-52-intent-parity.md)'s intent parity, the allocation budgets, the M1 gate's items in [Releases](../releases.md#m1-exit-gate) and the device rules in [Device sessions](../devices.md).

## Options rejected

- Ruling 3: refuse a canvas whose control moved to a worker, with an error that asks for a new canvas element. React components that render `<canvas ref>` under StrictMode would then fail on every mount in development.
- Ruling 6: accept the bytes under the crowd's allocation budget of 8 bytes a frame, with a recorded reason. The cause is not known yet, and the engine's rule is no allocation in the frames of a running scene.

## Consequences

- M2 gains two tasks: M2-J7 (texture packing) and M2-R21 (mesh and prefab destroy calls). M2-R15, M2-B7, M2-C9, M2-F7 and M2-E4 take the rulings that concern them.
- The gate's remaining iPad items run on the owner's iPad, after the GPU time regression is fixed.
- The device guides give Safari 18 as the minimum. The change that adds the start check gives it in the README and the user docs.
