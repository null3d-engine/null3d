# D-370: Two contract cases left with the frame record

Status: decided; the record states what the removal took. Task: none.

Summary: The planned contract held two cases against the directed frame record. One compared the
planned plant's bones and clumps with the frame's at the same seed. The other printed the fill
target against the frame's. The frame record left the tree, so both cases left with it. The
remaining contract cases hold the planned plant's own numbers. The sparseness contracts hold what a
caller reads. No case now compares a planned plant with a directed frame.

## Question

The planned model was built to replace the directed frame. Which checks of that replacement stand
now that the frame record is gone?

## Data

The two cases were A0 and F1 in the planned oak contract. A0 read the planned plant's axes of order
0 to 2 and its clumps against the frame grown at the same commit and seed. F1 printed the fill's
figures against the frame's clumps. Both needed a grown frame record. The frame record's grower left
with the frame model.

## Decision

Both cases are removed with the frame record. Their ground is held in part. The contract's remaining
cases hold the planned plant's heights, widths, leaf areas, sparseness steps and one-body shape. The
sparseness contracts hold the numbers a caller reads at five sparseness values. The frame-match check
was a migration check. It has no subject once the frame record is gone. No test now compares a
planned plant with a directed frame.

The deletion's story records this. The frame model's removal took two contract cases that measured
the planned model against the frame record. The remaining cases hold the planned plant's own
numbers.
