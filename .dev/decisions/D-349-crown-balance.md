# D-349: Judge a crown's balance either side of its trunk

Status: decided; the row and the measure are written into the guides, and the measure is being built. Task: the vegetation
rebuild's twelve-row standard.

Summary: Row 1 now judges how a crown's mass balances either side of the trunk, banded at ten hundredths against the
reference's own split. The owner set the alder's drawing beside its photograph and saw a tree carrying its foliage on one side
with the other hollow, where the photograph is even; no row saw it, because the shape overlap compares two outlines as areas,
and a lopsided crown can match an even one's area exactly.

## Question

Should the standard name balance, and should row 1 carry a figure for it? A builder that places its shoots by a rule can
produce a crown that is correct in every part and still leans, and the eye reads that lean at once.

## Rule

A row whose fault a viewer sees at a glance needs a figure, or a species passes a scorecard while a photograph beside it shows
the difference. So row 1 takes the balance figure, banded against the reference's own split rather than at an even half, so a
species that is normally one-sided is not pushed toward a shape its photographs do not have.

## Data

| Measure | Passing band | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crown balance, the covered area either side of the trunk's column | Within 0.10 of the reference's split, at every whole view | The owner's comparison of the common alder's still 02: the photograph carries its mass evenly, the drawing a full left flank and a hollow right one, while its shape overlap reads 0.594 |
| The same figure at the whole views of the species already judged | to be measured | The measure is being built; each species' rounds are re-read on row 1 when it lands |

How the data was produced: the owner's comparison of the alder's still 02 pair; the outline figures are the review tool's own.

## Look

The alder's still 02 decides it. The photograph's crown is one even dome. The drawing's is a fan: the foliage gathers on the
left, the right flank falls away into a hollow, and a viewer sees a tree leaning. Every figure in the standard passed it before
this decision.

## Consequences

- Row 1 takes a second figure, and a species judged before this decision is re-read on it at its next round.
- The measure is owed: the review tool computes each side's share of the crown's covered area at the trunk's column, for the
  render and the reference, and reports it beside the shape overlap.
- A species that is normally one-sided names it in its record, as the coverage rule already requires; the reference's own split
  is then the mark.
