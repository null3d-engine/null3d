# D-334: The vegetation review's rulings for the first batch

Status: decided by the owner. Rulings 1 and 3 need no build. The measure fixes of ruling 2 are pending.
Ruling 4 changes the batch's scope. Task: M3-V4.

Summary: Four rulings, taken together, to stop the review spending rounds on walls it cannot move. The
far-crown shading is an accepted limit for this batch. The two measure faults are fixed before any
figure is frozen again. The tree fern keeps its grown crozier and records the spiral as a limit. The
eight new species wait until the twelve existing ones are finished.

## Question

The review of twenty species kept re-judging rows it had already judged. It also kept failing rows that
no species change could move. The owner settled four questions:

1. The far-crown shading that fails row 10 on several species.
2. Whether to fix two known measure faults and re-read the batch once, or freeze today's measure.
3. The tree fern's crozier, whose spiral needs a per-axis texture.
4. Whether to finish the twelve existing species first, or keep all twenty in play.

## Rule

- **The far-crown shading is an accepted limit for this batch.** A row whose only fault is that shading
  passes on the owner's acceptance. The engine change becomes the next engine task, not a species task.
  The acceptance covers that cause alone, and any other fault still fails the row.
- **Measure faults are fixed before figures are frozen.** One finder takes part of a thin crown. One
  rule steps the sky gap at a foot-width threshold instead of scaling it. Both are fixed, then the batch
  is re-read once. Figures recorded before that re-read are provisional.
- **The tree fern keeps its grown crozier.** The spiral and the relief are stated limits. The cost of
  the texture that would carry them is recorded beside them.
- **The batch is the twelve existing species.** Scots pine, Italian cypress, date palm, giant sequoia,
  olive, saguaro, bamboo and tree fern record their state and stand down. They resume when the twelve
  existing species are complete.
