# E1430: Too many reflection probes

## What happened

scene.createReflectionProbe() would make a 17th reflection probe, and a scene has at most 16. Or the scene's first probe needs 6 views of the scene for its captures. The scene and its passes share 32 views, and its passes left fewer than 6.

## How to fix it

Destroy a probe that you no longer need before you make another, or let one probe's box cover more of the scene. Probes blend where their boxes overlap, so a few large boxes with a blend distance often look as good as many small ones. For the views, remove a scene pass or a reflection pass before you make the first probe.

## Example

```text
E1430: scene.createReflectionProbe() would make reflection probe 17; a scene has at most 16.
```
