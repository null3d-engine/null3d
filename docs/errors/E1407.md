# E1407: Invalid hold time

## What happened

The ?hold= switch or the hold option of createEngine gave a hold time that is not a number of seconds from 0 to 600.

## How to fix it

Give the sketch time to hold at in seconds, such as ?hold=1.5 or hold: 1.5. A bare ?hold holds at the time of the hold option, or at 0 without one.

## Example

```text
E1407: ?hold=1500ms is not a number of seconds from 0 to 600.
```
