# E1108: Value out of range

## What happened

A call received a count or an index past its limit, such as a row past the capacity of an instance batch.

## How to fix it

Keep counts and indices within the capacity you created the batch with, or create a larger batch.

## Example

```text
E1108: setActiveCount() got 1200, above the limit of 1000.
```
