# E1221: Invalid levels of detail

## What happened

`mesh.setLevels()` received levels that the mesh cannot take, or a glTF file's MSFT_lod levels could not become them. Each level gives one of an error, a distance and a coverage, and the switch distances grow from level to level.

<details>
<summary>Technical details: the rules for each level</summary>

A mesh takes at most 15 lower levels. Each level gives exactly one of an error, a distance and a coverage, as a finite number above 0. Its switch distance must lie farther than the level before it, and a coarser level covers more of the screen, so its coverage is smaller.

Each level must be another live mesh of the same engine, with the same vertex attributes as the base mesh. A level's material, where it gives one, must be a live material of the same engine. The `fov` option sets the reference view, and lies between 0 and 180.

`mesh.buildLevels()` refuses a mesh with joints, morph targets or a vertex record, whose shape poses or moves.

</details>

## How to fix it

List the levels from the most detailed down, each with a larger error, a farther distance or a smaller coverage than the one before, at most 15 of them. Make each level from the same kind of arrays as the base mesh, with the same attributes, such as the same texture coordinates and tangents. Give a mesh that poses or moves its levels with setLevels, not buildLevels.

[Where problems appear](../guides/debugging.md#where-problems-appear) says where each kind of error reaches your code.
