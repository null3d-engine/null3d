# E1224: Material cannot draw a mesh

## What happened

scene.overrideMaterial received a sprite, point or line material. Such a material draws the rows of its batch, or a sprite or line of its own, rather than the surface of a mesh, so it cannot stand in for every mesh of the pass.

## How to fix it

Give scene.overrideMaterial a material that draws meshes, from materials.standard(), materials.unlit(), materials.shader() or materials.decal(), or null to draw each mesh with its own material.

[Where problems appear](../guides/debugging.md#where-problems-appear) says where each kind of error reaches your code.

## Example message

The engine prints the part below, then the fix above and a link to this page.

```text
E1224: scene.overrideMaterial got a sprite material. It takes a material that draws meshes, from materials.standard(), materials.unlit(), materials.shader() or materials.decal().
```
