# E1217: Invalid material option

## What happened

A material factory or scene.createLines() got an option value that it does not take, or two options that cannot go together. The message names the option and the rule it breaks.

<details>
<summary>Technical details: the rules for each material option</summary>

A material factory or scene.createLines() received an option value that it does not take, such as an unknown alpha mode, blending or line mode. Custom materials refuse the 'weighted' blending, and take alpha to coverage or the hash mode only where their WGSL sets or reads the surface's alpha. A material that lets light through refuses the hash alpha mode and the weighted blending.

A layered material, with a clear coat, a sheen, iridescence or anisotropy, refuses a light map, specular maps, and two textures for a pair of layer maps that glTF packs into one. It refuses a transmission or a thickness map only where one of its layer maps fills the array that the map samples: the clear coat's normal map and iridescence's thickness map fill the array that the transmission map samples, and the sheen's maps and the anisotropy map fill the array that the thickness map samples.

A material with transmission refuses a thickness map beside a specular color map, whose sRGB texture never shares the thickness map's array, and other materials refuse those two maps.

A material that lets light through to its other side refuses the specular maps, whose places its diffuse transmission maps take, a clear coat, a sheen and the weighted blending, and other materials refuse the diffuse transmission maps. A custom material refuses subsurfaceRadius, which only a standard material takes. A custom material whose WGSL declares a coverage function takes the mask mode, or the blend mode with the cutoff that its shadow caster tests.

</details>

## How to fix it

Give alphaMode 'opaque', 'mask', 'hash' or 'blend', and blending 'normal', 'additive', 'multiply' or 'weighted'. three.js's transparent: true is alphaMode: 'blend', and its alphaTest is alphaMode: 'mask' with alphaCutoff. For a conflict between two options, follow the message, or read the rule for each kind of material on the E1217 docs page.

[Where problems appear](../guides/debugging.md#where-problems-appear) says where each kind of error reaches your code.
