# E1217: Invalid material option

## What happened

A material factory or scene.createLines() received an option value that it does not take, such as an unknown alpha mode, blending or line mode. Custom materials also refuse the alpha options that they do not take.

## How to fix it

Give alphaMode 'opaque', 'mask', 'hash' or 'blend', and blending 'normal', 'additive' or 'multiply'. three.js's transparent: true is alphaMode: 'blend', and its alphaTest is alphaMode: 'mask' with alphaCutoff. A custom material takes 'opaque', 'mask' or 'blend', and no alphaToCoverage.

## Example

```text
E1217: materials.standard() got the alpha mode "cutout"; it takes 'opaque', 'mask' or 'blend'.
```
