# E1217: Invalid material option

## What happened

A material factory or scene.createLines() received an option value that it does not take, such as an unknown alpha mode, blending or line mode, or an alpha option that custom materials do not take.

## How to fix it

Give alphaMode 'opaque', 'mask' or 'blend', and blending 'normal', 'additive' or 'multiply'. three.js's transparent: true is alphaMode: 'blend', and its alphaTest is alphaMode: 'mask' with alphaCutoff.

## Example

```text
E1217: materials.standard() got the alpha mode "cutout"; it takes 'opaque', 'mask' or 'blend'.
```
