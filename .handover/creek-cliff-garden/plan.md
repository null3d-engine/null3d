# Creek: a garden on a cliff above the fog

**Status: a plan in development. Do not implement yet.** Written against the milestone branch `m3-start` at `a3606f16` on 2026-11-02. The milestone has since moved on and merged into `main`, so re-check every finding below against `main` before relying on it. Nothing in this plan has been built.

## Context

Creek becomes the full demo of the vegetation add-on, wind and weather, all generated in code, with dense planting. The owner replaced its valley with a new setting and chose a look:

- A rough circular cliff, like a stump, with fog just below the rim that hides the ground.
- A creek across the diameter, falling over the edge at both ends.
- A big Buddha on one side, away from the creek and higher than it, reached by steps.
- A very beautiful garden over the rest of the top. Boulders and stones are generated by the add-on, which keeps its name.

**Chosen look:** green bronze in an autumn garden (job 2b3da0a9-dbff-44af-b766-4f0113f73ad2).

**Concept picture:** `concept.png` beside this file, the chosen look, made with Higgsfield (model `gpt_image_2_5`, 16:9, 1344 by 752).

The four other concepts stay in the owner's Higgsfield gallery and are not in git: bronze at sunrise (09848ab5-4aa6-4222-b3ff-1b982a62bd42), stone terraces in a tropical garden (00b0bad0-0e31-4156-b5dc-f45dcd49822e), white granite in spring with the livelier creek (6d9afe7a-50b1-4d6c-816c-82ea63841227), and gold at dusk (a9162a07-a005-4971-b4eb-a38d8baa106c).

## Design

### 1. Land and cliff (`creek/land.ts`)

- **Shape:** the height function becomes a raised disc with a noisy rim, about 60 to 70 m in radius, inside today's 160 m square. A terrace rises about 8 to 10 m above the water for the statue, with a channel across the middle. The water stays at height zero, so rule tolerances that read height above water still mean what they say.
- **Terrain add-on for the top:** detail near the camera, and four layers that fit the look exactly: moss, raked gravel, rock and wet bed. Raked lines come from a generated gravel map.
- **Walls:** try `steepFaces`, which landed, first. A height field cannot overhang, so if blocks stretch on the near-vertical faces, add a generated wall mesh in dark volcanic rock.
- **Check:** close pictures of the bed, and the ground in the stream's reflection.

### 2. Fog and the world's edge (new `creek/fog.ts`)

- **Removed:** the mountain ring, the gorges, the gorge haze and the ring's far cards. This removes the cards' draw and bake cost.
- **The engine's fog does not cover the background,** so fog alone leaves the plain background below the horizon. Add a floor at fog height reaching the horizon in the fog's color, plus soft drifting sheets at the rim. `examples/lib/haze.ts` shows how to take the air's color from the stage each frame, but its shape suits a gorge, so write a ring version.
- **Manual camera:** it stays above the fog's top, and bounds change to suit. This needs the ring exemption recorded in D-170 and `.dev/examples.md`, as the Island has, and the test that names scenes keeping no ring needs the entry.

### 3. Water (`packages/water`, `creek/sketch.ts`)

- **Creek:** one river shape across the diameter, using the existing bed and current functions. With a spring-fed pool near the middle, the current changes direction there.
- **Livelier water** if the owner agrees to borrow from concept 4 (job 6d9afe7a-50b1-4d6c-816c-82ea63841227): steps in the bed, larger boulders and more foam through the existing `foamMap`.
- **New waterfall in the water add-on,** so other scenes can use it: a falling sheet at the rim, spray from the particles add-on, and mist where it meets the fog.
- **Reflection:** only plants near the water go in it, using the existing unreflected layer.

### 4. Rocks in the vegetation add-on

- **New `packages/vegetation/src/rock.ts`:** a record of numbers and a seed gives a rock. Cut a sphere with random planes for hard faces, add ridged noise, squash, and flatten the underside. It reuses the level format and the worker task. Built-in records: boulder, stone, pebble.
- **Rock material:** detail projected from world space with no seams, moss on whichever side faces up, tint and lichen per row, and a wetness value that darkens and glosses. Row colors now survive on coarser levels.
- **Placement:** a new first layer, `rock`, in `ecology.ts` and `scatter.ts`. Plants avoid rocks by default. Clusters can gather around any layer, so stones gather around boulders. This replaces today's `rocks` mask in `valley.ts`. A built-in rule set gains no rock rules, so its checksum holds.
- **Hand-placed composition rocks** go in as fixed points before the scatter.
- **`creek/stones.ts`** moves to these rocks, and still feeds foam from the points. Other demos keep the old helper for now.

### 5. Species (`packages/vegetation/src/builtin.ts`)

- **New records:** maple, sculpted pine and azalea. Clipped shrubs come from the existing shrub record with a tighter crown. Ferns and hostas stay.
- **Maple color:** try the per-copy tint for red to orange. If its range is too narrow, use two records.
- **Sculpted pine:** today's whorls make tiers but not flat cloud layers, so the crown needs a new shape option. This is early test 2.
- **Wildflower record:** dropped, since this look does not call for it.
- **Memory:** Creek's set must stay under the ceiling, so these replace willow, oak, beech and birch. Keep four variants, and check generation time at load.

### 6. Planting (`creek/valley.ts`, `creek/plants.ts`)

- **Garden rules:** written with `speciesRule`, not `builtinRules`, with tighter spacing than the picture shows.
- **Masks through `groundMaps`:** its existing `paths` option, which takes line shapes, keeps the steps and walks clear. It also blocks the terrace, the gravel, the view to the statue, and everything past the rim. The mask is needed because the beech, birch and broadleaf rules read no slope.
- **Keep:** `clearOfRoute` and its route test, `plantWind` and `setWindLayer`.
- **Remove:** `ringCards` and the fallen logs, which do not suit a tended garden.
- **No hand-over:** on a disc this small, every tree can stay a mesh with morphing levels. That avoids the owner's main complaint entirely. Check the cost from the wide orbit. Add cards only if the figures require them, and if so, follow the Island's method.

| Starting targets at Medium, to tune | Today (over 160 m square) | Garden (about 15,000 m²) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trees and saplings | part of 2,216 | about 800 |
| Shrubs, ferns, hostas, azaleas | part of 2,216 | about 6,000 |
| Boulders and stones | about 70 | about 1,500 |
| Grass tufts at High | 52,000 | up to 52,000, in patches |

**Grass:** the chosen ground is moss and gravel, so grass only needs the banks and some patches. Today's tufts packed into about 40% of the disc give about 9 per square meter, with no new triangles, about 3.4 M at High. So the garden no longer needs the GPU-built grass, and the old zone design goes. Moss close up needs low cushion plants, or it will read flat. Grass cost is about 9 ms on the Mac at High if it scales with count, which needs measuring against the add-on's 49 ms High allowance on Apple devices.

**Wind reach:** plants sway only within 8 to 32 m by preset, so far trees stand still in the wide view. The grass moves, and leaves fall through the whole view, which may be enough. Show the owner this view. A longer reach needs a ruling.

### 7. Statue and garden structures (`creek/statue.ts`, `creek/structures.ts`, new)

- **Statue:** blended smooth shapes meshed from a distance field, with a generated patina material, greener in the folds and streaked by rain. The repo has no such meshing, so this is new code, and it is the largest look risk.
- **Fallback:** the owner rules on one model file. D-288 already allows models for the Island's buildings.
- **Steps, roofed gate, stone lanterns:** generated. Lanterns glow at dusk and night through the existing moods. Steps wind up from the creek, then run straight to the statue.
- **Rule:** no overlapping geometry, tested with `findOverlaps` in `examples/lib/overlap.ts`.
- **Occluders** from coarse copies of the statue and terrace.

### 8. Camera and moods (`creek/route.ts`, `creek/sketch.ts`)

- **Route:** a brief wide orbit over the fog, past a waterfall, along the creek, through the gate and up the steps to the statue. It must pass the route limits in `examples/lib/showcase-routes.test.ts`, and the clearance test gains the statue, gate and walls.
- **Start list:** `lod: true`.
- **Moods:** soft overcast morning opens, and the others stay.
- **Air:** `air.ts` falling leaves in red and orange. `flora.ts` floating leaves match, and fireflies stay.
- **Bounce light:** the Island turned it off by ruling. Compare on and off here at High before deciding.

### 9. Storm

- **Controls:** a storm button through `stage.setWeather`, as Skyway does, with `wind.setBase` and `wind.gust` raising the air together.
- **Rain and splashes** through the weather add-on's calls.
- **Terrain gains a `wet` option.** Its surface ships compiled, so a page cannot add the effect. The wet block goes in a second compiled surface, chosen only on request, so a dry ground pays nothing. `wet.test.ts` holds the copy to its source. A setter updates rain and time with no allocation. Water depth fades out on steep ground. No mirror, to save cost, with a stand-in filling the texture slot.
- **Rocks** use their wetness value.
- **Water gains rain rings,** off by default.

### 10. Remove and document

- **Remove:** `createMountains`, `GORGE`, `createHaze`, `ringCards` and the logs from Creek. The shared helpers stay for other scenes.
- **Update:** `examples/demos.ts` (title and summary), the Creek section of `.dev/examples.md`, `.dev/sample-content.md`, D-170, D-241 (status line, which still lists M3-V11 and far trees as unbuilt, and a rocks addendum), `docs/api/vegetation.md`, `terrain.md`, `water.md`, the weather README and the `null3d-develop` skill.
